# Complete Indoor Cannabis Cultivation Calendar: Day-by-Day Milestones

Indoor cannabis cultivation follows predictable biological phases with specific intervention windows. This guide provides precise timing for every training technique, environmental adjustment, and harvest preparation step—designed to be directly translated into calendar events. The total grow cycle spans **12-20 weeks** depending on strain genetics and vegetative duration, with photoperiod plants offering complete control over timing.

## Seedling phase establishes the foundation (Days 1-21)

The seedling phase begins at germination and ends when plants develop their first **5-7 finger leaves**, typically around day 21—not the commonly cited day 14. Understanding daily progression prevents premature interventions that can stunt growth.

**Days 1-3: Germination**
Seeds placed in water or paper towels begin imbibition. The white taproot cracks through the seed shell within **24-48 hours**. Plant when taproot reaches **3-5mm**, positioning it facing downward.

**Days 3-5: Emergence**
Seedling pushes through soil. Cotyledons (round embryonic leaves) unfold and begin photosynthesis. Maintain **70-80% humidity** under a dome, with temperatures at **20-25°C (68-77°F)**.

**Days 5-7: First true leaves**
Single-finger serrated leaves appear at the first node. Remove humidity dome vents or crack the lid to begin hardening off.

**Days 7-10: Second node development**
Three-finger leaves emerge. Remove humidity dome completely. Target **65-70% RH**. Light intensity should remain gentle at **100-200 PPFD** using CFL or LED at distance.

**Days 10-14: Third node appears**
Five-finger leaves develop. This marks the earliest possible transplant window for photoperiod plants. First transplant is optimal when leaves extend beyond the container's circumference and roots are visible at drainage holes.

**Days 14-21: Late seedling/transition**
Multiple nodes developing rapidly. By day 21, plants should have **4-5 sets of true leaves** with the characteristic seven-finger pattern emerging. The seedling phase officially ends here.

| Day | Milestone | Environmental Target |
|-----|-----------|---------------------|
| 0 | Seed soak/paper towel | Dark, 70%+ RH |
| 1-3 | Taproot emergence | 20-25°C |
| 3-5 | Cotyledons open | 70-80% RH, dome on |
| 5-7 | First true leaves | Begin venting dome |
| 7-10 | Second node (3-finger) | Remove dome, 65-70% RH |
| 10-14 | Third node—transplant window | 100-200 PPFD |
| 14-21 | Late seedling, 4-5 leaf sets | Transition to veg |

**Seedling phase parameters:** pH **6.3-6.5** (soil) or **5.5-6.0** (coco/hydro). EC **0.4-0.8 mS/cm** (100-400 PPM). No nutrients needed—seed contains all required energy. Light schedule **18/6** throughout.

## Vegetative phase shapes plant architecture (Days 21-60+)

The vegetative phase is when growers actively shape plants through training techniques. Timing these interventions correctly maximizes yield potential while allowing adequate recovery before flowering.

### Training technique timing

**First topping: Day 28-35 (Week 4-5 from seed)**
Top when plants have **5-6 nodes**, cutting above the 5th node. The plant should be at least 30 days old with vigorous growth. Recovery takes **3-7 days**. For mainlining, top earlier at the 3rd node (around Day 21-28).

**Second topping: Day 42-49 (1-2 weeks after first)**
Once new growth tips are **2-3 inches long**, top each resulting branch above the 2nd or 3rd node. This creates 4-8 main colas. Allow **5-7 days** recovery between topping events.

**FIMming alternative: Day 28-35**
FIM (remove 70-80% of growth tip) can be performed slightly earlier than topping, at **3-5 nodes**. Recovery takes **7-14 days**. Results are less predictable but can produce **3-4+ new colas** versus topping's reliable two.

**LST initiation: Day 21-28 (Week 3-4)**
Begin low stress training when branches are long enough to bend, typically at **4-6 nodes**. Continue adjusting ties **every 2-3 days** throughout veg. LST can continue into early flower through Week 2-3.

**SCROG screen installation: Day 21-28 (Week 3-4)**
Install screen when plants reach **12-18 inches tall**, positioning it **8-20 inches** above pot base. Continue tucking branches horizontally until screen is **60-80% full** (indica) or **50-60% full** (sativa) before flipping to flower.

**Super cropping window: Day 35-49 (Week 5-7)**
Perform when stems are green and pliable, on plants at least **12 inches tall** with **3-4+ nodes**. Recovery requires **24-72 hours** initially, with full knuckle formation taking **up to 2 weeks**. Stop super cropping **3-7 days before flip**.

| Technique | Start Day | Node Count | Stop Before Flip | Recovery Time |
|-----------|-----------|------------|------------------|---------------|
| LST | Day 21-28 | 4-6 nodes | Week 2-3 flower | Ongoing |
| Topping | Day 28-35 | 5-6 nodes | 14 days | 3-7 days |
| FIM | Day 28-35 | 3-5 nodes | 14 days | 7-14 days |
| SCROG | Day 21-28 | 12-18" tall | Week 1-2 flower (tucking) | N/A |
| Super crop | Day 35-49 | 3-4+ nodes | 3-7 days | 24-72 hrs |
| Mainline | Day 21-35 | 5-6 nodes | 7-14 days | 5-7 days/cut |

### Transplanting schedule

**First transplant: Day 10-14**
Move from starter container (solo cup/1L pot) to 1-3 gallon container when 3 sets of true leaves develop. Wait **3-7 days** after transplanting before feeding.

**Final transplant: Day 21-35**
Move to final container (3-5 gallon for indoor grows) before intensive training begins. Complete all transplanting at least **2 weeks before flip** to allow root establishment.

**Autoflower exception:** Plant directly into final container (5-15L) to avoid transplant stress. Use LST only—avoid topping unless highly experienced.

### Vegetative phase parameters

Environmental targets shift as plants mature: **VPD 0.8-1.2 kPa** (50-60% RH at 22-28°C). Light intensity increases to **400-600 PPFD**. pH **6.0-7.0** (soil) or **5.5-6.0** (coco/hydro). EC climbs from **1.0-1.4 mS/cm** (early veg) to **1.4-1.8 mS/cm** (late veg), representing **300-700 PPM**.

Begin nutrient introduction **Day 21-28** at **25-50%** manufacturer strength, increasing weekly. NPK ratio for veg: approximately **3-1-2** (nitrogen dominant).

## Pre-flower preparation maximizes bud sites (Days -7 to Day 1 of Flower)

The week before flipping to 12/12 is critical for setting up flowering success. Specific interventions during this window dramatically impact final yield.

**Day -7 to -3 before flip: Heavy defoliation**
Remove large fan leaves blocking lower bud sites, focusing on leaves with long stems in the middle and bottom canopy. Target leaves covering potential bud sites or blocking airflow.

**Day -7 to -3: Lollipopping**
Remove bottom **1/3 to 1/2** of plant growth that won't reach the canopy. Keep only the top **4-5 nodes** per branch. This directs energy to top colas rather than larfy bottom buds.

**Day -3: Final training check**
Ensure all LST ties are positioned correctly. SCROG screens should be **50-80% full** depending on strain. Complete any final super cropping. All high-stress training must be finished to allow **minimum 3 days recovery** before flip.

**Day -1: Pre-flip preparation**
Verify environment is dialed: humidity can begin transitioning toward **50-60% RH**. Some growers give **24-48 hours of darkness** before first 12/12 cycle, though this is optional and not scientifically proven necessary.

## Flowering phase develops and ripens buds (Weeks 1-12)

Flipping to 12/12 light triggers the flowering hormone cascade. Each week brings distinct developmental milestones requiring specific interventions.

### Week 1-2: Stretch phase (Days 1-14 of Flower)

**Day 1: Flip to 12/12**
Switch lights from 18/6 to 12/12. The "stretch" begins immediately—plants may **double or triple** in height over the next 2-3 weeks. Sativas stretch more aggressively than indicas.

**Day 1 option: First schwazze**
The "Three A Light" technique calls for removing **all fan leaves with visible stems** below the top 2-3 nodes on Day 1. This extreme defoliation is controversial but can increase yields when combined with increased feeding.

**Days 1-7: Maintain veg nutrients**
Plants still need nitrogen during stretch. Continue with veg nutrients or use a **50/50 veg/bloom** transition mix. EC: **1.2-1.4 mS/cm** (600-700 PPM).

**Days 8-14: Peak stretch**
Height gains are most dramatic. Continue tucking SCROG screens. LST can still be performed to control height. White pistils begin appearing at nodes (preflowers). This is when sex becomes identifiable—remove any males immediately.

**Week 1-2 environment:** Temperature **68-75°F** day, **65-70°F** night. Humidity **50-60% RH**.

### Week 3: Transition and second defoliation (Days 15-21)

**Day 15-19: Stretch slowing**
Vertical growth decelerates. "Budlets" (small clusters of white pistils) become visible. Switch fully to bloom nutrients at **100%** strength.

**Day 20-21: Second schwazze / heavy defoliation**
This is the **final opportunity** for aggressive leaf removal. Remove fan leaves covering bud sites and lower growth not receiving direct light. After Day 21, avoid removing any significant foliage—the plant has shifted energy allocation to bud production.

**Day 21: Stop SCROG tucking**
Allow colas to grow vertically **10-12 inches** above the screen from this point forward.

### Week 4-6: Bud development and peak nutrient demand (Days 22-42)

**Week 4 (Days 22-28): Bud formation**
Distinct bud structures develop at each node. Calyxes (tear-drop shaped structures) become visible. Trichomes begin developing. Increase bloom nutrients to **100-110%** strength.

**Week 5 (Days 29-35): Bud fattening begins**
Daily size increases become visible. Pistils remain mostly white, pointing outward. EC peaks at **1.6-2.0 mS/cm** (800-1000 PPM). This is **peak nutrient demand**—plants require maximum phosphorus and potassium.

**Week 6 (Days 36-42): Continued fattening**
Buds gain significant mass. Begin reducing nitrogen while maintaining high P-K. Light defoliation permitted—only remove leaves **directly laying on buds**. Odor becomes potent. Support stakes or yo-yos may be needed for heavy branches.

**Optional Day 42 cleanup:** Minor leaf removal if needed, targeting only leaves directly blocking light to buds.

**Week 4-6 environment:** Reduce humidity to **40-50% RH** to prevent mold in dense bud structures. Maintain airflow without direct wind on buds.

### Week 7-8+: Ripening and harvest preparation (Days 43-56+)

**Week 7 (Days 43-49): Begin monitoring trichomes**
For 8-week strains, begin checking trichomes **2-3 times per week** using **60-100x magnification**. Pistils start darkening and curling inward (50-70% orange/brown). **Begin flush for soil grows** (7 days before target harvest).

Nutrient intensity drops to **75-100%** bloom (EC **1.0-1.5 mS/cm**). Some fan leaf yellowing is normal and expected—the plant is redirecting mobile nutrients to bud production.

**Week 8 (Days 50-56): Harvest window opens for fast strains**
Trichomes should be mostly cloudy/milky. Continue daily trichome monitoring. Begin flush for **coco (3-5 days out)** or **hydro (2-3 days out)**. Pistils **70-90%** darkened.

**Week 9-12: Extended flowering for longer strains**
Sativa-dominant and some hybrid strains require **10-14 weeks** of flowering. Continue monitoring trichomes. Flush timing remains the same relative to harvest date.

| Flowering Week | Day Range | Key Events | Nutrients | Notes |
|----------------|-----------|------------|-----------|-------|
| Week 1 | 1-7 | Stretch begins, continue veg nutes | 75-100% veg | Flip to 12/12 |
| Week 2 | 8-14 | Peak stretch, transition nutes | 75% bloom + 25% veg | Pistils appear |
| Week 3 | 15-21 | Heavy defol Day 20-21, stretch ends | 100% bloom | Stop SCROG tucking |
| Week 4 | 22-28 | Bud formation | 100-110% bloom | Peak demand starting |
| Week 5 | 29-35 | Bud fattening | 110-120% bloom | Maximum P-K |
| Week 6 | 36-42 | Continued fattening | 100-120% bloom | Optional light defol |
| Week 7 | 43-49 | Begin trichome checks, start flush (soil) | 75-100% bloom | Begin ripening |
| Week 8+ | 50-56+ | Harvest window | Flush | Daily trichome checks |

## Determining precise harvest timing requires multiple indicators

Harvest timing is the single most consequential decision affecting final product quality. Multiple indicators should align before cutting.

**Trichome color is the primary indicator**
Using **60-100x magnification**, examine trichomes on calyxes (not sugar leaves, which mature faster):

- **Clear/transparent:** Too early—low potency, harsh smoke
- **Mostly cloudy/milky:** Peak THC—energetic, cerebral, creative effects
- **Amber/golden:** THC converting to CBN—sedative, body-heavy, "couch lock"

**Target ratios based on desired effect:**
- **Uplifting effect:** 80-90% cloudy + 10-20% amber
- **Balanced effect (most common):** 70% cloudy + 20-30% amber
- **Sedative effect:** 50-60% cloudy + 40-50% amber

**Secondary indicators confirm readiness:**
Pistils should be **70-90%** darkened and curled into buds. Fan leaves display natural yellowing while sugar leaves on buds remain green. Calyxes appear swollen and plump. Buds feel dense with noticeable heft.

**Strain-specific timing differences:**
Fast indicas finish in **7-8 weeks**, standard indicas/hybrids in **8-10 weeks**, and sativas may require **10-14+ weeks**. Always rely on plant indicators rather than breeder timelines, which represent ideal conditions.

## Post-harvest processing preserves quality (Days H+1 to H+60+)

Proper drying and curing are arguably more important than growing technique for final smoke quality. Rushing these stages destroys terpenes and creates harsh, grassy-tasting product.

### Drying phase (Days H+1 to H+14)

**Harvest day: Cut and hang**
Wet trim large fan leaves immediately if in humid environment (>60% RH). Dry trim (wait until after drying) preserves terpenes better in dry climates (<50% RH). Cut branches and hang with adequate spacing—buds should not touch each other.

**Drying environment is critical:**
- Temperature: **60-70°F (16-21°C)**
- Humidity: **50-60% RH**
- Light: **Complete darkness** (UV degrades THC)
- Airflow: **Gentle and indirect** (oscillating fan pointed at wall, never directly at buds)

**Drying duration: 7-14 days**
Target **10-14 days** for optimal terpene preservation. Faster drying (5-7 days) produces harsher smoke. Adjust based on environment:

| Environment | Adjustment | Duration |
|-------------|------------|----------|
| Very dry (<40% RH) | Leave more leaves on, consider paper bags | 12-14+ days |
| Normal (50-60% RH) | Remove fan leaves, hang branches | 7-10 days |
| Humid (>60% RH) | Trim thoroughly, use drying racks, dehumidifier | 5-7 days |

**Stem snap test determines completion:**
When small stems **snap cleanly** (rather than bending) and buds feel dry on the outside but aren't brittle or dusty, drying is complete. Buds should "pop off" stems cleanly without stringy fibers.

### Curing phase (Weeks 1-8+)

**Day H+10-14: Begin cure**
Transfer dried buds to wide-mouth glass mason jars, filled **75%** capacity. Place in cool, dark location at approximately **70°F (21°C)**.

**Week 1: Critical monitoring period**
- **Days 1-3:** Burp jars **2-3 times daily** for **10-15 minutes** each session
- **Days 4-7:** Burp **1-2 times daily** for **5-10 minutes**
- Check humidity with mini hygrometers inside jars
- If ammonia smell detected, remove buds immediately and dry further
- If buds feel moist/sticky after closing jar, leave lid off **1-4 hours**

**Target jar humidity: 55-62% RH**
- Over 70%: Too wet—dry more
- 62-70%: Slightly wet—leave lids off 1-4 hours
- **55-62%: Cure zone** ✓
- Under 55%: Too dry—add Boveda pack

**Weeks 2-3: Reduced burping**
Burp **once daily** for a few minutes, or every other day for 10-15 minutes. Strain's true terpene profile emerges. Smoke smoothness improves noticeably.

**Week 4+: Maintenance phase**
Burp **2-3 times weekly**. After 4 weeks, cure is essentially complete for most purposes. Add **Boveda 58% or 62% packs** (size 8 for quart jars) for humidity stabilization if desired—add after Week 1, not before.

**Weeks 6-8: Optimal quality**
Peak flavor and smoothness achieved. Some strains continue improving with extended cure of **2-3+ months**. For long-term storage, burp **once monthly** and store in cool, dark conditions.

| Cure Week | Burping Frequency | Duration | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------|----------|-------|
| Week 1 | 2-3x daily | 10-15 min | Critical monitoring |
| Week 2 | 1x daily | 5-10 min | Profile developing |
| Week 3 | Every other day | 10-15 min | Smoothness improving |
| Week 4+ | 2-3x weekly | Few minutes | Add Boveda optional |
| Week 6-8+ | 1x weekly/monthly | Quick check | Storage mode |

## Complete grow calendar template

For an 8-week flowering strain with 5-week veg, the total cycle spans approximately **15 weeks** from seed to cured product:

**Weeks 1-3:** Seedling phase—germination through 4-5 node development
**Weeks 3-8:** Vegetative phase—training, transplanting, canopy development
**Weeks 8-16:** Flowering phase—stretch, bud development, ripening, harvest
**Weeks 16-18+:** Post-harvest—drying and curing to optimal quality

The precise timing of every intervention—from first topping at Day 28-35 to second schwazze at Day 21 of flower to beginning flush 7 days before harvest—creates the framework for a successful grow. Individual strains and environments will require adjustment, but these evidence-based timelines provide the foundation for predictable, repeatable results.

## Conclusion

Successful indoor cannabis cultivation depends on precise timing of interventions aligned with plant biology. The **seedling phase (Days 1-21)** requires patience—no training, minimal handling, gentle environment. The **vegetative phase (Days 21-60+)** is the intervention window, with first topping optimally performed at Day 28-35 and LST beginning at Day 21-28. **Pre-flower preparation (Days -7 to -1)** maximizes bud sites through strategic defoliation and lollipopping. The **flowering phase** follows a predictable 8-12 week timeline with heavy defoliation permitted only through Day 21, flush beginning 7 days before harvest (soil), and trichome monitoring starting Week 7. **Post-harvest processing** requires 10-14 days of drying followed by minimum 4 weeks curing with decreasing burp frequency. Converting these milestones to calendar events creates a roadmap that eliminates guesswork and maximizes genetic potential.
